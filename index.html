About Me

Hey! I'm Namish Dawar, a Class X student from Gurugram who loves turning ideas into things that actually work — whether that's embedded code on an Arduino, a 3D-printed mechanism, or a web app.

My flagship project, the Parkinson's Self-Stabilizing Spoon, earned 1st Runner Up at KVRSS India in the Physics category — a device designed to help people with hand tremors eat independently.

I'm drawn to projects that sit at the edge of engineering and empathy — where code and hardware come together to solve real problems. With 17 repositories and counting, I'm always building something new.

Parkinson's Self-Stabilizing Spoon

A hardware-software fusion project: an Arduino-powered spoon with gyroscopic stabilization to counteract hand tremors in Parkinson's patients. Includes embedded control code (.ino) and a fully 3D-modeled physical structure (.stl). Researched tremor behavior patterns, designed stabilization algorithms, and created a complete functioning prototype.
